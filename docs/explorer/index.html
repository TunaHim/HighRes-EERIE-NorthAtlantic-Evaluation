What the indices and options mean

Wettest day (RX1day): the largest one-day rainfall in the year or winter. Rain on very wet days (R95p): total rain on days wetter than the local 95th percentile of wet days (wet day: at least 1 mm). Share of rain on very wet days: R95p as a percentage of all wet-day rain. Hottest day (TXx): the highest daily maximum temperature.

Difference from a reference: percent for the rainfall indices, °C for TXx. Orange means the dataset is wetter or warmer than the reference, blue drier or cooler. Correlation with the winter NAO: how consistently the winter index rises in positive-NAO winters (detrended, 29 winters); models use their own NAO, E-OBS and ERA5 the ERA5 NAO. Values beyond about ±0.38 are individually significant at the 5% level.

Regional averages use the same land cells for every dataset (land where E-OBS has data).

Maps are averaged to 0.5° for this page; the notebooks use the full 0.25° grid. Data and methods: see the notebooks and the methods page. The provenance of the data behind this page (code version, inputs, date) is stored in data.json.
